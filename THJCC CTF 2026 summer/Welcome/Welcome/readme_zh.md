Please follow the rules and do not violate them!

Competition Rules

1. Participants must not leak, disclose, or share any solutions, hints, or answers during the competition.

2. Participants must not collaborate with external parties during the competition, including but not limited to having others solve challenges on their behalf or accepting hints or assistance from others.

3. Do not carry out any destructive or unauthorized attacks against the organizer’s infrastructure, including but not limited to DDoS attacks, directory/path scanning, or port scanning, unless explicitly required by a challenge.

4. Violators will be subject to penalties depending on the severity of the violation. Serious violations may result in disqualification from both the competition and prize eligibility.

5. If you encounter technical issues during the competition, please contact the organizers through the official Discord server or by email. Private inquiries to challenge authors or staff members will not be accepted.

6. If, during or after the competition, you discover that another participant may have violated the rules above, or that a staff member may have engaged in improper participation or other violations, please email admin@thjcc.org directly and provide supporting evidence.

7. Prize claim rules will be announced separately in the Discord server. Please join the official server to avoid affecting your eligibility or rights.

8. In the event of any dispute arising from these rules, the organizers reserve the right of final interpretation and decision.
