# Intruder

## 題目描述

I was talking with my friend Jman and he was telling me about his 21 year old discovery.

It was very crazy and he told me I was a skeptic, just like justinLife and other people.

Who found this discovery? Format: boroCTF{username}

## 解題思路

題目中的關鍵字有幾個

```text
Jman
21 year old discovery
justinLife
```

先從 Jman 和 justinLife 這兩個看起來像使用者名稱的線索下手搜尋，可以找到 Project SKYBOX / Interloper 相關的 2005 年 Half-Life 討論串

該討論串的發文者是 `|DeV1RTU0S0|x`，即為 flag

## Flag

```text
boroCTF{|DeV1RTU0S0|x}
```
