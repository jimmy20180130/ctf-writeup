# The Noise That Stayed

## Description

A stripped crop shows part of a real scientific instrument at a protected historic site. The original upload had coordinates, but they were removed.

Identify the instrument, the hill/locality tied to the site, and the discovery made with it. Normalize as: 0xV01D{instrument_hill_discovery}
